## Intro

Rain, snow, or dark winter months shouldn't mean a traveling skater has to sit still. Indoor parks make it possible to skate year-round regardless of weather — but unlike outdoor parks, they often open and close on short notice, change hours seasonally, or require membership. This article lists a large set of **verified** indoor parks around the world, each backed by at least one reliable source, with contact details where publicly available. The list is **not exhaustive** — new parks open and old ones close constantly — but it's a solid, fact-checked starting point for planning a trip.

## Summary checklist

- Always confirm current opening hours directly with the park — they often change seasonally.
- Some parks require membership, not just a drop-in fee.
- Status can change fast — several well-known parks turned out to be closed during this research (Skatelab, House of Vans London, KC Indoor Skatepark, CA Training Facility) despite appearing in older articles or directories.
- Don't trust crowd-sourced directories or AI-generated summaries blindly — always verify against at least one independent source.
- Call or email ahead for longer trips.
